# Invasive-tumor footprint decrease

Read `references/primitive_contract.json`. This primitive removes invasive
tumor tissue and regenerates the newly exposed non-tumor population. It is not
the inverse switch of a growth skill and does not claim pCR, RCB improvement or
patient benefit. The edit must form one visually meaningful, coherent retreat
from a single invasive component; scattered low-area boundary nibbles are not a
valid footprint decrease. The domain mechanism owns the clinical scenario and
geometry.

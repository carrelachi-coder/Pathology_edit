# Cell-type abundance increase

Resolve the requested class explicitly, allocate an incremental count/extent budget, and place only complete instances in a legal host zone. A moderate abundance increase must add at least 12 complete source-calibrated nuclei across at least 4 spatial foci spanning at least 6 local nucleus diameters; a microscopic one-cluster insertion is not a successful edit. Preserve all other populations outside the bounded support. Read [primitive_contract.json](references/primitive_contract.json).

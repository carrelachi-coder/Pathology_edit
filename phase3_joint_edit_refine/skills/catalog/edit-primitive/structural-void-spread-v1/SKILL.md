# Structural-void spread

This primitive is reserved for mechanisms whose receiving space is a native
void or luminal compartment rather than stroma. The structural auxiliary map
must prove the void and its relationship to the primary lesion. Tissue labels
remain unchanged unless a future dataset-specific contract explicitly labels
the void contents.

The deterministic containment, complete-footprint capacity and
separation-from-primary contracts are implemented and regression-tested. The
primitive remains fail-closed until externally produced native-void and
protected-structure maps are digest-bound and a three-arm frozen-generator
ablation demonstrates a real condition response. Never infer these maps from
the source H&E merely to make the primitive executable.

# Structural-void spread

This primitive is reserved for mechanisms whose receiving space is a native
void or luminal compartment rather than stroma. The structural auxiliary map
must prove the void and its relationship to the primary lesion. Tissue labels
remain unchanged unless a future dataset-specific contract explicitly labels
the void contents.

The primitive is catalogued but fail-closed until void containment, separation
and generator-response gates have completed internal validation.

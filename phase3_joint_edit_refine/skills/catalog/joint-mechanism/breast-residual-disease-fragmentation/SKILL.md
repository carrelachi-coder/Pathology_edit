# Breast residual-disease fragmentation

Read `references/joint_contract.json`. Require explicit residual-disease and
post-treatment context. The executor may split only the existing invasive
component and must satisfy the skill-owned residual island contract. Mature
target-population regeneration and its local-density audit remain required,
but a traversing stromal corridor does not require a newly generated stromal
nucleus at every interface-anchor segment. The tissue program must use an
organic branching corridor with nonconstant width and spend remaining turnover
on peripheral retreat of every residual focus; straight parallel slots are a
visual veto even when component-count gates pass.

# PANDA operational tumor-to-stroma turnover

Require explicit post-treatment semantic intent and fine-9/10 source authority. Fine-2 is only operational Stroma: it does not prove fibrosis, tumor bed, pCR, response, prognosis or clinical benefit.

`invasive-tumor-footprint-decrease-v1` may retreat only a certified solid,
lumen-distant external tumor boundary. Preserve every detected lumen-associated
epithelial wall pixel-exactly; partial wall thinning, notching or transection is
an execution veto. PANDA does not expose a separate generic stromal-increase
primitive because that label transition duplicates tumor-footprint decrease.
